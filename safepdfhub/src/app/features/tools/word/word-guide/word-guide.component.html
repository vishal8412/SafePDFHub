Editable text

Keep selectable PDF text in positioned, editable text boxes over the original artwork. For scanned pages, choose the document language so OCR can attempt to read the words.

IN WORDMake it your own
  • Good for revising clear text
  • OCR: English, Hindi and Marathi
Check before you share

Text boxes preserve placement but do not reflow like ordinary paragraphs. Fonts may differ. Uncertain scans and scanned tables stay as images. Proofread recognized text.

I want to keep the page look

Keep page appearance

Place each PDF page in Word as an image, keeping the original content and layout visible together.

IN WORD
The whole page, together
  • Good for forms and complex layouts
  • Keeps tables, stamps and handwriting visible
Know what you’ll get

Text within page images is not editable. Image-based Word files can be larger than the original PDF.
