ABOUT SAFEPDFHUB

PDF tools built around privacy.

SafePDFHub is a privacy-first PDF toolkit for everyday document work, with a strong preference for processing files in your browser whenever the technology supports it.

OUR STARTING POINT

Your files are yours. We build the tools around that principle.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Four principles.

The product decisions behind how we build SafePDFHub.

01

Local-first

When a tool can run in your browser, we prefer that architecture over sending the document to a server.

02

Simple by default

PDF work should feel direct. We aim for focused workflows instead of unnecessary complexity.

03

Honest product claims

We distinguish what is processed locally, what requires a service, and what is still being developed.

04

Built for real documents

Compatibility, visual fidelity, accessibility, and predictable exports matter as much as a polished interface.

WHY WE'RE BUILDING IT

Useful PDF software doesn't have to mean uploading your files.

Many PDF tasks are small: merge a few pages, compress a document, protect a file, add a signature, or make a quick edit. SafePDFHub is being built for those moments—with a strong preference for processing work in the browser whenever the technology allows it.

That approach can give people more control over their documents while keeping the product fast and convenient. We focus on practical PDF tools, clear interfaces, and engineering that makes privacy part of the product rather than an afterthought.
