ABOUT SAFEPDFHUB

PDF tools built around privacy.

SafePDFHub is a privacy-first PDF toolkit built to make everyday PDF work simple without treating your documents like something that needs to leave your device.

OUR STARTING POINT

Your files are yours. The tools should work around that principle.

WHY WE'RE BUILDING IT

Useful PDF software doesn't have to mean uploading your files.

Many PDF tasks are small: edit a line, merge a few pages, compress a document, convert a file, or make a quick correction. SafePDFHub is being built for those moments—with a strong preference for processing work in the browser whenever the technology allows it.

That approach gives people more control over their documents while also making the product fast and convenient. We are focused on practical tools, clear interfaces, and engineering that keeps privacy in the product rather than adding it as an afterthought.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Four principles.

01

Local-first

When a tool can run in your browser, we prefer that architecture over sending the document to a server.

02

Simple by default

PDF work should feel direct. We aim for focused workflows instead of unnecessary complexity.

03

Honest product claims

We will clearly distinguish what is processed locally, what requires a service, and what is still being developed.

04

Built for real documents

Compatibility, visual fidelity, accessibility, and predictable exports matter as much as a polished interface.
