PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Important: This policy is written for SafePDFHub's current privacy-first, browser-based product model. Before commercial launch, replace any business/contact details that differ from the final setup and have the policy reviewed for your actual services, analytics, payments, and legal entity.

1. Our approach

SafePDFHub is designed around a simple principle: your files are yours. Where a feature is implemented for local browser processing, the PDF or other document is processed on your device rather than uploaded to a SafePDFHub file-processing server.

Because the exact data flow can differ by feature and may change as the product evolves, we aim to describe those changes clearly rather than make a blanket promise that every future feature will always be local.

2. Files and documents

For browser-local tools, your selected files are read by the web application in your browser. We do not intentionally receive those document contents through the website's application servers.

Do not assume a feature is local if the interface explicitly says that a third-party service or remote processing is required. We will identify such requirements where applicable.

3. Information you provide

If you contact us, you may provide information such as your name, email address, subject, and message. The current Contact page prepares an email using your device's email application; it does not submit the form contents to a SafePDFHub form-processing server.

If you later use a payment service or another hosted service, that provider may collect information necessary to provide its service. Its own privacy policy will apply to information it processes.

4. Automatically collected information

Web hosting, security, performance, analytics, advertising, or other infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request timestamps, or diagnostic data if those services are enabled.

SafePDFHub should only publish this policy as a final statement after its production hosting, analytics, advertising, cookies, and third-party integrations have been audited. Any enabled service will be reflected in the production privacy notice.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The current product is intended to avoid unnecessary tracking. If cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising, authentication, or similar technologies are introduced, their purpose and relevant choices will be documented here and in any required cookie notice.

6. Third-party services

Some product infrastructure may rely on third-party libraries or services. A browser library used to process a document locally is different from a service that receives the document on a remote server. We will distinguish these cases in the relevant product documentation.

7. Security

Local processing can reduce the need to transmit document contents, but no website or device can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your browser, operating system, and security software up to date and use only trusted devices for sensitive documents.

8. Children's privacy

SafePDFHub is not intentionally directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when SafePDFHub's features, infrastructure, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the current version was published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the Contact page. Before production launch, confirm that the public privacy contact address and the legal identity of the operator are accurately published.
