Decision gate

This ledger is an evidence collection tool, not an automatic promotion system. qpdf remains a prototype until repeated results across representative PDF complexity classes and device classes are reviewed. Import exported JSON from other test machines when building a broader evidence set.

  • Do not increase public file/total/page limits from a single benchmark.
  • Do not treat equal output byte size as a fidelity requirement.
  • Investigate every failed parse, fidelity mismatch, encrypted-input result, and malformed-input result.
  • Review responsiveness and stability in addition to raw throughput.

Operational rules
