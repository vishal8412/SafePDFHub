Interpretation

  • Open measures PDF.js document-open time.
  • Render sequentially renders every page and validates a WebP preview is produced.
  • Export reopens the source with pdf-lib and writes one native text field per page.
  • Peak heap is available only in browsers exposing performance.memory.
  • Max event-loop gap is a responsiveness signal, not a formal browser-performance SLA.
  • These results are measurements, not promises about every device.
